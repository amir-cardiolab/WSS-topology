About

Method of Arzani, Gambaruto, Chen & Shadden, Wall shear stress exposure time: a Lagrangian measure of near-wall stagnation and concentration in cardiovascular flows. Fixed points are located from the Poincaré index of each triangle and the zero of the linear field inside it; manifolds are the trajectories leaving the saddles along their eigenvectors. Source code and the Python package: github.com/amir-cardiolab/WSS-topology.
