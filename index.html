About

Method of Arzani, Gambaruto, Chen & Shadden, Wall shear stress exposure time: a Lagrangian measure of near-wall stagnation and concentration in cardiovascular flows (BMMB 2017); WSS fixed points: Arzani & Shadden (J. Biomech. 2018); WSS LCS: Arzani, Gambaruto, Chen & Shadden (JFM 2016). Fixed points are located from the Poincaré index of each triangle and the zero of the linear field inside it; manifolds are the trajectories leaving the saddles along their eigenvectors; the streamlines are evenly spaced integral curves of the WSS direction field. Source: github.com/amir-cardiolab/WSS-topology.
